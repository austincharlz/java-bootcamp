# Lab 33 — Component Inventory

## Step 1 — Screen

Imagine a Customer list showing Amina and Ravi with status badges.

## Step 2 — Inventory

5 components: `App`, `CustomerList`, `CustomerCard`, `StatusBadge`, `PageHeader`.

## Step 3 — One responsibility
App: The root component that renders the entire application.
CustomerList: Displays a list of customers, each represented by a CustomerCard component.
CustomerCard: Represents an individual customer, displaying their name and status badge.
StatusBadge: Displays the status of a customer (e.g., Active, Inactive) in a visually distinct way.
PageHeader: Displays page title and actions
